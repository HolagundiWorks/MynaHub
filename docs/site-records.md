# Live company site records

Implemented 2026-11-16 for the company API and Mobile, using Hub's existing shared record kinds: instruction, inspection and snag. Desktop company-file records and Mobile local practice records remain separate; no automatic migration or upload occurs. Daily site reports, inspection-specific photos, issue-event workflows and permits are separate modules and are not silently mapped into these records.

Records contain the calendar record date, title, details, location, person responsible as record text, optional due date, status (open/resolved/closed), optional resolution date and revision. A resolved/closed record requires a resolution date on or after the record date; open records have no resolution date. Date and field bounds are checked in the API, database and Mobile. Responsible text does not grant project membership or notify another person.

GET/POST /v1/projects/:projectId/site-records and PATCH/DELETE /v1/projects/:projectId/site-records/:id provide scoped reads and online mutations. Reads require current project read scope; sites.write permits create/edit and sites.delete permits confirmed deletion. Owner, Administrator, Principal and Project Manager receive both by default. Site Supervisor receives sites.write only, within assigned-project scope. Custom write/delete roles require projects.read.

Mobile's View company site records opens independently paginated live pages of at most 25 records on a currently visible project. The same authenticated long poll carries siteProjectId/siteAfter and a nullable sitePage. Forms and rows stay in RAM and clear on backgrounding, leaving, disconnecting or lost scope. Local practice records are unchanged.

Saves use stable operation/record UUIDs and expected revisions for edits. Conflicts require explicit review and a new operation. Deletion requires typing DELETE and a fresh login; it retains a tombstone and audit history. Deleted records disappear from live pages and cannot be restored by retrying creation. Receipts are immutable, actor/company scoped, and commit with the record and audit. Raw runtime writes to records remain denied; controlled functions enforce scope and protected identity.
