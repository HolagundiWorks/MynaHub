# MynaHub

MynaHub is the company hub of the Myna suite: company sign-in, users and roles, practice records (clients, projects, letters, invoices, accounts, bank import, tax, staff, library, proposals, meetings, attendance), estimates and bills, approvals, the company database (`.osas`) and the only cloud sync (Neon, paused for now).

MynaHub has **no AI**, and there is **no launcher**. It starts MynaOps, MynaCAD and Myna3D by path and passes nothing.
