# Invoices and accounts

Open these from **Billing**, **Books** and **Reports and tax** in the menu. **Billing** holds Invoices and receipts and Bank reconciliation. **Books** holds Expenses, Income, Salaries, Assets and Journals. **Reports and tax** holds Financial reports, Tax, and Chart and financial years.

## Invoices

Choose **Create** for a draft. Save it, then issue it when the figures are final. An issued invoice keeps its PDF.

- **Convert to tax invoice** turns an issued pro forma invoice into a tax invoice.
- **Cancel invoice** cancels an issued invoice that has no payments. A reason is required.
- Record a receipt with its date and amount. Receipts can include TDS.

The GST scheme printed on invoices comes from **Office → Firm**: not registered, composition, or regular.

## The books

| Page | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| Financial reports | Choose a date range and open a report. |
| Journals | List posted journals for a period. A posted journal stays as posted. A correction is a reversal. |
| Expenses | Record practice expenses. |
| Income | Record rent, interest, and other income. |
| Salaries | Record salary payments. |
| Assets | Record assets the firm owns and depreciation for the year. |
| Tax | Record GST, TDS, and other tax payments, and read the GST and TDS abstract for a period. |
| Chart and financial years | Review the chart of accounts and close the selected financial year. |

Use the accent button on each form to post or save. **Refresh** reloads a list after a change.

## GST and TDS abstract

On **Reports and tax → Tax**, below the payment form, choose a period (it starts at the financial year) and press **Show abstract**.

- **GST:** for CGST, SGST, IGST and composition tax, what was owed at the start of the period, what was charged in it, what was paid to the government, and what is owed at the end. A payment that was later reversed is taken out of "paid"; it is not counted as new tax charged.
- **Input tax credit:** the credit available at the start, added, used, and available at the end.
- **TDS deducted by clients:** by section, the number of receipts, the amount received and the TDS deducted. This is the credit the firm claims. Receipts that were reversed are left out.
- **TDS payable by the firm:** TDS the firm deducted and what it has paid.
- **Other statutory dues:** income tax, provident fund, ESI and professional tax, when they have figures.
- **Tax payments in the period**, each with the account it was paid from.

**Export CSV** saves the same figures to a file that opens in Excel: amounts are plain numbers with two decimals and no currency sign, and each section has its own heading row. The abstract reads posted records and changes nothing.
