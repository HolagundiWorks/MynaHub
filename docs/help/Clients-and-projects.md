# Clients and projects

Open these from **Practice** in the menu.

## Clients

Choose **Create** to add a client. Fill the form and save. **Edit** on a row opens that client again.

**Archive** hides a client from everyday work and keeps the record. Issued documents that already name the client stay as they were issued.

## Projects

Choose **Create**, pick the client, and set the **Consultancy type**. The list follows the Council of Architecture Conditions of Engagement:

- Comprehensive architectural services
- Interior architecture
- Landscape architecture
- Urban design and urban renewal
- Site development
- Architectural conservation
- Retrofitting of buildings
- Graphic design and signage

The project list shows the consultancy type beside the project. **Edit** changes the record. **Archive** keeps it without deleting it.

## Pulse

**Pulse** is the practice home. It shows the work that needs attention. **Refresh** reloads it. Use **Ctrl+K** when you want to jump to another page.
