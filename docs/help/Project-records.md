# Project records

These records sit with a project. None of them posts a journal or issues a letter on its own.

## Stages

A project has a consultancy type and a number of floors. Together they choose a standard set of stages: the phase marker and the intended payment schedule for a new or draft agreement. More than one floor splits the working-drawings share across the floors, and the last floor takes the remainder.

Marking a stage records the phase and shows whether the matching payment is due. It does not post a journal and does not create an invoice. An issued agreement keeps the milestones it was issued with.

## Time book

Hours are recorded against a project. A time line is a record of time only; it does not post a journal.

## Transmittals

A transmittal is the log of drawings and documents sent on a project: date, recipient, purpose, medium and reference, then a line for each drawing (number, title, revision, copies). Mark a transmittal acknowledged when the recipient confirms. Cancelling a line removes it from the list. A transmittal does not replace an issued letter.

## Reports and presentations

A project report or a presentation (a 16:9 slide deck) is a record of what was shown to the client. Images and PDF annexures are stored inside the company file, so they travel with it and are included in backups. Drawing marks on a slide are stored relative to the slide, not in pixels.

## Client and project statements

The client statement, the project fee position and the project report read records already in the company file. Each client and each project has its own ledger in the reports; accounts receivable remains the control account.

## Writing off an uncollected bill

An invoice that will not be collected can be written off with a date, an amount and a reason. The write-off is posted to the **Bad debts written off** account (5960). Invoices already issued are not edited.

## Client details

Besides the billing identity and authorised representatives, a client records its kind (individual, company or architect firm), legal name, city and state, TAN, and GST registration (regular, composition, unregistered or SEZ).
