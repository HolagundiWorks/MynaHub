# Enquiries, site records and permits

These three pages sit under **Practice** in the menu. They are plain records. None of them posts to the books.

## Enquiries
Record each person who asks about work: when they asked, how they found you, what they want, and a follow-up date. An enquiry whose follow-up date has arrived shows "(due)". Choose **Make client** on a line to add the person to Clients; the enquiry is marked Won and this is done only once.

## Site records
Keep snags, site instructions and inspections against a project. Give a snag a due date and a person responsible. It shows **Overdue** until you set its status to Resolved or Closed. Choose **Edit** on a line to change it.

## Permits and approvals
Record each statutory approval for a project: the authority, your reference, and the dates. A status other than Planned needs the application date, and Approved or Rejected needs the decision date. Approvals show "(soon)" within 60 days of their valid-until date and "(lapsed)" after it.

## Staff, leave and the library
Under **Office**. **Staff** is the roster; give a leaving date when someone leaves and they stay for past records. **Leave** records requests and approvals: days run from the first day to the last day inclusive, and a person cannot have two leaves on the same day. **Library** keeps specifications, standards, compliance notes and lessons learned.
