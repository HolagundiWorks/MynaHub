# O-Studio logo options

The shape is unchanged from the original mark. Only the colours differ. **Option A (Amber and Ink) is the chosen logo** and is what the app, the window icon and the installer icons use (native/Adms.Desktop/Assets and src-tauri/icons were made from brand/option-a). Option B is kept for reference.

## Option A: Amber and Ink

Keeps the yellow identity. The letter is now ink on amber, so it holds in black and white and at 16 pixels. On dark surfaces the mark drops its tile: an amber letter with a deep-amber bar. On white the amber tile edge is soft (1.6:1), so the ink letter carries the shape; use the one-colour mark where a hard edge is needed.

| Colours | Standard (light surfaces, app icon) | Reversed (dark surfaces) |
|---|---|---|
| Roles | tile #FFC20E, letter #17181B, bar on letter #FFFFFF, bar on tile #17181B | tile none, letter #FFC20E, bar on letter #6B4F00, bar on tile #FFC20E |

Contrast ratios (WCAG; 3:1 is the minimum for graphics, 4.5:1 for text):

- standard: letter on its ground: 11.0:1
- standard: bar on the letter: 17.8:1
- standard: bar on the ground: 11.0:1
- standard: name on its surface: 17.8:1
- standard: tile edge against the surface: 1.6:1  (below 3:1)
- reversed: letter on its ground: 10.4:1
- reversed: bar on the letter: 4.7:1
- reversed: bar on the ground: 10.4:1
- reversed: name on its surface: 15.0:1

## Option B: Teal and Terracotta

A calmer, more architectural palette: a deep teal tile with a warm paper letter and terracotta bars. On dark surfaces the tile turns to warm paper so it stays a bright, readable shape.

| Colours | Standard (light surfaces, app icon) | Reversed (dark surfaces) |
|---|---|---|
| Roles | tile #0F4C5C, letter #F6F1E7, bar on letter #C2410C, bar on tile #F6A27A | tile #F6F1E7, letter #0F4C5C, bar on letter #F6A27A, bar on tile #C2410C |

- standard: bar on the letter: 4.6:1
- standard: bar on the ground: 4.7:1
- standard: name on its surface: 9.5:1

- reversed: letter on its ground: 8.4:1
- reversed: bar on the letter: 4.7:1
- reversed: bar on the ground: 4.6:1
- reversed: name on its surface: 15.0:1
- reversed: tile edge against the surface: 15.0:1
